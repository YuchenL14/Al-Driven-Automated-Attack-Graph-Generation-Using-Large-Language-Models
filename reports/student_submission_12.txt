An attacker accessed an online marketplace selling breached credentials
(T1589.001; mitigation M1056). The source does not state that any credential
from that marketplace was obtained or used against Transport for London.

Two attackers infiltrated the Transport for London network between 31 August
and 3 September 2024. The source does not state how entry was achieved, so no
technique is given for this step.

The attackers accessed data held in Transport for London's Oyster refunds
system (T1213; mitigations M1047, M1041, M1018, M1032).

Customer refunds were delayed, leaving some customers out of pocket for longer
than usual.

The Oyster photocard application system for children and young people was
closed.

All 28,000 Transport for London employees were required to attend an office in
person to reset their passwords.

Transport for London reported GBP 29 million in loss and recovery costs.
