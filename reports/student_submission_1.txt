On 22 March 2023, a malicious file was unintentionally downloaded onto a Capita employee's device. The file enabled malicious software to be deployed on Capita's network. Although a high-priority security alert was raised within ten minutes, the device remained unquarantined for 58 hours, during which the attacker exploited Capita's systems.

The attacker maintained access, gained administrator permissions, moved laterally across multiple domains, accessed other areas of the network, and compromised critical systems. Capita had not implemented a tiering model for administrative accounts, which enabled the privilege escalation and lateral movement.

Between 29 and 30 March 2023, nearly one terabyte of data was exfiltrated. The stolen information included pension records, staff records, customer records, criminal records, financial data, and other special-category data. The breach affected the personal information of 6.6 million people and impacted 325 organisations.

On 31 March 2023, ransomware was deployed on Capita's systems. The attacker reset all user passwords, preventing Capita staff from accessing their systems and network.
