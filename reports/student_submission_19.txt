Attackers first gained access to Marks and Spencer systems as early as
February 2025. The report does not state how that access was achieved, so no
technique is given for this step.

The attackers stole the NTDS.dit file from the Windows domain controllers
(T1003.003; mitigations M1026, M1027, M1041).

The Active Directory database containing password hashes for all domain
accounts was in the attackers' possession.

The attackers deployed DragonForce ransomware against VMware ESXi hosts on
24 April 2025 (T1486; mitigations M1040, M1053).

Company servers and the virtual machines running critical business
applications were encrypted.

In-store contactless payments were disrupted.

Online and app-based shopping was suspended, and Click and Collect order
processing was paused.

Operations at the Castle Donington warehouse were impacted and around 200
agency staff were told not to attend shifts.

Approximately 700 million pounds was wiped from the company's market value.
