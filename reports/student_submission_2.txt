From 19 February 2022, evidence suggested that a threat actor made repeated brute-force attempts against the sqluser administrator account. There were approximately 400 attempts across thirteen incidents. The report did not establish that these attempts successfully provided access.

On 3 June 2022, a threat actor likely compromised an end-user laptop and used it to authenticate to DPP's network. The compromise allowed the threat actor to access the sqluser account.

The sqluser account belonged to a legacy case-management system. It did not use multi-factor authentication and had unrestricted administrator access across DPP's network.

After gaining access, the threat actor ran PowerShell commands. Indicators showed that Cobalt Strike was deployed on the network.

The threat actor used the compromised sqluser account to move laterally across DPP's network.

On 4 June 2022, logs showed that Windows Defender was disabled and a virtual-machine backup service was stopped. Forensic investigators believed that the threat actor then deployed ransomware.

MegaSync and Rclone were installed during the incident. The threat actor used tools to exfiltrate data from DPP's network.

Towards the end of the incident, the threat actor used the sqluser account to download and run antivirus software as a clean-up action, making the investigation more difficult.

DPP's email server stopped working and staff lost access to the network. Files across DPP's servers were corrupted, and DPP's systems did not operate properly for approximately one week.

The National Crime Agency later confirmed that three folders containing 32.4 GB of DPP data had been published on the dark web.
